This directory runs Chocolate Doom compiled to WebAssembly.

Engine: Chocolate Doom, GNU GPL v2 — https://github.com/chocolate-doom/chocolate-doom
Port:   cloudflare/doom-wasm, GNU GPL v2 — https://github.com/cloudflare/doom-wasm
        built from commit 65e0d3a with updated Emscripten link flags
        (see EMFLAGS in configure.ac; build with scripts/build.sh).

doom1.wad is the unmodified DOOM shareware episode (v1.9), (c) id Software,
distributed under its original shareware terms.
